A SIMPLE WAY TO MOVE FORWARD

From intention to action.

  1. Describe a goal. Add your timeline and constraints.
  2. Review the roadmap. Each action is visible before approval.
  3. Approve the plan. Approval updates the task states; nothing external is changed.
  4. Track your progress. Mark tasks complete and inspect the activity log.

LifePilot uses a guided planning workflow and a persistent task backend. It does not connect to your calendar or send messages.
